An Introduction to Derivatives
intro

Think of driving: your speed is how fast your position changes. The derivative captures exactly that idea for any function, at any instant.

the limit

Formally, the derivative is the slope of the line between two points as they slide together:

f'(x) = \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x+h) - f(x)}{h}
power rule

Apply the power rule to x^3. Take your first try.

hint1

Not quite. For x^n, the n moves in front. Keep that in mind and try again.

attempt2

With the exponent moved to the front, what do you get?

hint2

Still off. The 3 comes down as a coefficient and the new exponent is 3 - 1 = 2.

attempt3

mastered

The power rule in action: coefficient 3, exponent lowered to 2. The same move handles any power of x.
