Nested Circles - a Layer 2 demo lesson

Let's work through this problem together:

A target is made of nested circles. If the radius of the outer circle is 5, what does the region look like, and how much of it is shaded?

Don't rush, we'll build the idea up one piece at a time.

Here's our strategy: instead of guessing, we'll break the shape into pieces we already understand, measure each piece, and add them back up. Small steps, no jumps.

Picture 1 out of 4 of the whole. The shaded part of the bar below is exactly that fraction.

Here is the circle x² + y² = 5² drawn live. Drag and zoom it to get a feel for how the radius controls the size.

And that's the whole idea. We broke the shape into pieces, measured each one, and added them up, the same move works on much harder problems too. Nice work.
